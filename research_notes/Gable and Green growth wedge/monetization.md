# Monetization reality (US, 2025–2026) for a home-decor / small-space / outdoor-living content site

Research date: 2026-11-09. All figures are US unless stated. Dates are flagged on every threshold because 2025–2026 saw major changes at every ad network (Raptive Oct 2025, Mediavine Jan 2026, Ezoic Feb 2026). Where a figure comes from an affiliate-tool directory (Lasso, FlexOffers, UpPromote, LinkClicky) rather than the merchant or network, it is marked as such — those directories conflict with each other and should be confirmed in the network dashboard before relying on them.

## Key Question 1: Exact current thresholds and rules — Journey by Mediavine, Mediavine, Raptive (and Rise), Ezoic, SHE Media; AI-content, Pinterest-share and new-domain restrictions

### Takeaway
As of late 2026 the ladder is: Journey by Mediavine at 1,000 Tier-1 sessions/30 days (since 15 Jan 2026, site ≥4 months old, Grow plugin running 30 days), Raptive at 25,000 pageviews/month (since 16 Oct 2025, ≥50% Tier-1 traffic, domain ≥6 months, Rise retired), Mediavine "Official" at $5,000/yr ad revenue (no session minimum), SHE Media at ~20,000 pageviews, and Ezoic effectively closed to small sites at 250,000 monthly users (since 19 Feb 2026; "Incubator" for smaller sites). No network publishes a Pinterest-share cap, but all three premium networks say they review traffic source mix and demand "clean, human" traffic and original, human-involved content; Mediavine and Raptive both actively terminate sites for AI-content violations.

### Cited Findings

**Journey by Mediavine**
- Official requirement: "a minimum of 1,000 sessions from Tier 1 countries (including the U.S., Canada, the U.K., and Australia) within a 30-day period," plus "original, audience-first content" and meeting "content quality and traffic standards" — [Mediavine requirements page](https://www.mediavine.com/?p=19304)
- Mediavine help center: "over 1,000 sessions" to start Journey; application also reviews "traffic countries of origin, traffic sources, reader demographics, and a range of additional quality and safety checks" — [Mediavine Help: What does it take to get approved](https://help.mediavine.com/what-does-it-take-to-get-approved-by-mediavine)
- The 1,000-session threshold took effect 15 January 2026, replacing the 10,000-session threshold Journey launched with in March 2024; Grow plugin must be installed and running 30 days before evaluation; site must be at least four months old; Mediavine verifies traffic is human, not purchased/bot; content must be original, brand-safe, regularly updated — [Productive Blogging: Journey guide (2026)](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/)
- Journey revenue share: publisher gets 70%, Mediavine 30%; paid Net 65 — [Productive Blogging: Journey guide](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/)
- Journey launched March 2024 "starting at 10k sessions" (historic baseline) — [r/Blogging thread, Mar 2024](https://lr.in.psf.lt/r/Blogging/comments/1bq83h9/mediavine_journey_offering_starts_at_10k_sessions)
- Journey is described by Mediavine's own support community as self-supported (no account manager) — [Journey Zendesk community](https://journeymv.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/community/posts/35413184458907--Inquiry-Regarding-Stagnant-RPM-and-Earnings-Despite-Increased-Traffic)
- Third-party summary adds "connected GA4 account" and says Grow is required for display, though not listed in the official requirements — [Webscraft](https://webscraft.org/blog/google-adsense-vs-journey-by-mediavine-scho-obrati-dlya-svogo-saytu?lang=en) (third-party; unverified against Mediavine)

**Mediavine (Official and above)**
- Official requirements: "minimum of $5,000 in annual ad revenue," "original, audience-first content," "clean, human, brand-safe traffic," good standing with Google AdSense and AdExchange, reader experience that supports premium advertising — [Mediavine requirements page](https://www.mediavine.com/?p=19304)
- Productive Blogging estimates the $5,000/yr threshold equates to roughly 25,000 pageviews/month; Mediavine notifies Journey publishers approaching it and begins additional review — [Productive Blogging: Journey guide](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/)
- New revenue-share table for sites launched after 1 Jan 2026: Official (<$100K annual ad revenue) 75%; Select ($100K–$249,999) 80%; Signature ($250K–$499,999) 85%; Premiere ($500K–$999,999+) 90%; Premiere Plus ($1M+) 90%. Sites launched before 1 Jan 2026 follow a legacy calculation — [Mediavine Help: Revenue Share](https://help.mediavine.com/revenue-share)
- A legacy Pro publisher reports ~90% share (85% + 5% tenure) under the old system and says the new system pays less "at every level"; also "Mediavine has 6 tiers in total" — [Productive Blogging newsletter: What is going on with Mediavine and Raptive](https://productive-blogging.kit.com/posts/what-is-going-on-with-ads-at-the-moment)
- Older Mediavine standard (now superseded for new sites): 50,000 sessions in the previous 30 days per Google Analytics — [The Website Flip: analysis of 1,193 Mediavine sites](https://thewebsiteflip.com/guide/mediavine-analysis/) (pre-2026 figure — outdated)
- Mediavine AI policy (7 Mar 2024, still the operative statement): does not monetize "low-quality, mass-produced, unedited or undisclosed AI content that is scraped from other websites"; "will not tolerate publishers using AI to create untested recipes"; developing automated detection; vetted sites that degrade will be caught and face "immediate action"; AI permitted for assistive uses (e.g., alt tags) — [Mediavine: AI and Our Commitment to a Creator-First Future](https://www.mediavine.com/?p=70240)
- Mediavine terminated multiple publisher accounts 6 Mar 2024 for excessive AI content, flagged by third-party content-quality tools and confirmed by internal review — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/mediavine-terminates-publisher-accounts-over-ai-generated-content-concerns/); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/mediavine-bans-publisher-for-overuse-of-ai-generated-content/526343/)
- Mediavine announced layoffs; CEO Eric Hochberger: "AI-powered search and shifting traffic patterns are putting real pressure on publishers" — [AdExchanger daily roundup, 4 Feb 2026](https://www.adexchanger.com/daily-news-roundup/thursday-02042026/)
- Chartbeat data cited by Mediavine: Google Search pageviews to publishers fell 34% and Google Discover 15% between Dec 2024 and Dec 2025 — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/mediavine-names-advocacy-director-as-google-page-views-fall-34/)
- Pinterest-heavy traffic: Mediavine reps have told applicants they want "diversified traffic"; bloggers report Mediavine prefers "stable organic traffic that's coming from Google" — [Pommie Travels](https://pommietravels.com/how-to-get-approved-for-mediavine/) (secondhand anecdotes, not policy)

**Raptive (formerly AdThrive) and Raptive Rise**
- Raptive lowered its minimum to 25,000 monthly pageviews (from 100,000); Rise pilot retired and folded into "Insider," the first creator level; Insider creators can add more sites once they reach 25,000 pageviews; referral bonuses $1,000 (100K+ pageview sites) and $250 (25K–100K, through 31 Jan) — [Raptive blog: Opening the door to more creators (updated 16 Oct 2025)](https://raptive.com/blog/opening-the-door-to-more-creators-who-meet-raptive-quality-standards/)
- Geography: sites at 25,000–99,999 pageviews need ≥50% of traffic from US/UK/Canada/Australia/New Zealand; sites at 100,000+ need 40%; Rise had launched in 2024 for 50K–100K pageview sites; change effective immediately for new applications — [Search Engine Journal, 20 Oct 2025](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/)
- Domain must be at least six months old; GA must be configured correctly; Raptive looks for long-form content on most pages and "meaningful human involvement" in content — [PPC Land: the new math behind premium ad network approvals](https://ppc.land/is-your-site-finally-ready-the-new-math-behind-premium-ad-network-approvals/)
- Raptive's announcement warns that "AI can inflate pageviews on low-quality sites," which is part of why it moved to a lower pageview floor plus stricter quality screening — [Raptive blog](https://raptive.com/blog/opening-the-door-to-more-creators-who-meet-raptive-quality-standards/)
- Raptive reported denying or removing over 590 sites in 2025 for AI-related violations (51 existing members, 539 rejected applicants) — reported via [search summary of a Dec 2025 Raptive press release](https://techintelpro.com/Marketing/Content-Management/raptive-surpasses-6300-sites-with-record-2025-growth) (page could not be fetched directly; figure is company-reported and unverified here)
- Raptive network: 6,500+ sites, $4B cumulative payouts; 5,600+ creators adopted its "Terms of Content Use" anti-AI-scraping framework (launched Oct 2025) — [NetInfluencer, 25 Feb 2026](https://www.netinfluencer.com/digital-media-company-raptive-crosses-4b-in-publisher-payouts/)
- Raptive's AI-content policy help article exists but returned HTTP 403 to fetch — [Raptive Help: AI-generated content policy](https://help.raptive.com/hc/en-us/articles/23945568132123-What-are-Raptive-s-policies-around-AI-generated-content) (text not retrieved)
- Raptive revenue share is widely cited as 75% — [Newor Media (competitor blog)](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1430) (third-party)

**Ezoic**
- Official: sites "generally required to have 250,000+ monthly active users"; publishers monetizing before 19 Feb 2026 are grandfathered if continuous; grandfathered sites under 250K lose status if Ezoic is removed >7 days; new sites added to existing accounts after 19 Feb 2026 must meet 250K; smaller sites may apply to the "Ezoic Incubator Program"; must comply with Google policies incl. "reliable traffic sources," no "automatically generated content," no scraped content; AdSense approval not required for most sites — [Ezoic Support: Getting Started — Ezoic's Requirements](https://support.ezoic.com/kb/article/getting-started-ezoics-requirements); [Ezoic Incubator page](https://www.ezoic.com/incubator)
- This replaced the no-minimum "Access Now" era (Ezoic had dropped its 10,000-visit floor in 2021/2023 depending on source) — [Newor Media](https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1430); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/)

**SHE Media**
- Minimum 20,000 pageviews/month per SHE Media's own site; SEJ (Oct 2025) also lists ~20,000 — [SHE Media Collective explained](https://shemedia.com/audience-development/she-media-collective-explained); [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/)
- One blogger reports SHE Media accepted sites under 5,000 pageviews, so the floor is soft in practice — [Imperfect Idealist review](https://imperfectidealist.com/she-media-review/)

**Comparative survey RPMs by network (all niches, Jan–Apr 2025, n=187)**
- Raptive $34.86; Mediavine $31.55; Journey $11.15; Ezoic $11.08; SHE Media $8.03; Monumetric $6.77; AdSense $2.34 — [Productive Blogging: Blogging Income Survey 2025](https://www.productiveblogging.com/how-much-do-bloggers-earn-2025/)

### Inferences
- For a brand-new home/outdoor site, the realistic 2026 ladder is: AdSense or nothing (months 1–4) → Journey at month 4–6 (needs 4-month site age, Grow installed 30 days, 1,000 Tier-1 sessions) → Raptive at 25K pageviews once the domain is 6 months old → Mediavine Official once Journey revenue hits $5K/yr (~$417/mo, roughly 25K pageviews at Journey's RPM — in practice more like 35–40K pageviews given Journey's ~$11 RPM).
- Ezoic is no longer a realistic option for a new site (250K users); SHE Media at 20K pageviews is the only sub-25K alternative to Journey, with a materially lower survey RPM ($8 vs $11).
- No network publishes a Pinterest-share cap. Both Raptive's Tier-1 geography rule (50% US/UK/CA/AU/NZ) and Mediavine's "Tier 1 sessions" rule are the only hard traffic-source-adjacent gates. A Pinterest-heavy site passes those easily (Pinterest skews US) but will be reviewed for engagement and "diversified traffic."
- AI-generated content is the single biggest rejection reason at Raptive (539 applicants rejected in 2025). Human editing, disclosure, and demonstrable "meaningful human involvement" are table stakes for both premium networks.

### Gaps
- Raptive's AI-content policy text was not retrievable (403). The "590 sites" enforcement figure comes from a search summary of a Dec 2025 press release that could not be fetched.
- Mediavine does not publish a definition of "premium sessions" / "Tier 1" beyond listing US/CA/UK/AU as examples.
- AdThrive→Raptive rebrand date (April 2023) is widely known but no source was fetched in this session confirming it; treat as unverified here.
- Journey's placement in the new 2026 revenue-share table is not on Mediavine's official page; the 70% figure is from Productive Blogging.

## Key Question 2: Realistic RPM ranges (home/garden) at each tier, by season, and Pinterest vs Google sessions

### Takeaway
Best available 2025 data puts Home Décor at ~$36 average RPM for established bloggers on premium networks, with Journey averaging ~$11 and AdSense ~$2; DIY/home-improvement benchmarks run $7–16 (typical) to $24+ (top decile) in another 450-blog dataset, and lifestyle/home Q4 indexes at roughly 1.6–2.4× Q1. There is no authoritative Pinterest-vs-Google RPM split; Raptive says Pinterest RPM has improved from its "viral pin, high bounce" past, bloggers report both directions, and a Pinterest-only site reports ~$8–10 RPM rising to ~$20 once even <10% Google traffic was added.

### Cited Findings

**RPM by niche / tier**
- Home Décor average RPM $36.23 (all revenue streams of bloggers who named it their niche; survey of 187 bloggers, Jan–Apr 2025); overall ad RPM $33.80 — [Productive Blogging: Blogging Income Survey 2025](https://www.productiveblogging.com/how-much-do-bloggers-earn-2025/)
- Average Journey RPM $11.15 (survey), with "some publishers reporting $30+" — [Productive Blogging: Journey guide](https://www.productiveblogging.com/everything-you-need-to-know-about-journey-by-mediavine/); "Journey average RPM $11.50" — [Productive Blogging newsletter](https://productive-blogging.kit.com/posts/what-is-going-on-with-ads-at-the-moment)
- DIY/Home Improvement typical $7–16, top-10% $24+; Lifestyle/General (includes home decor) $4–12, top 10% $18+; by network AdSense $3–12, Ezoic $8–20, Mediavine $15–40+, Raptive $18–50+; dataset "450+ blogs on Mediavine, Raptive, Ezoic, Jan 2025–Mar 2026," display ads only, US/UK/CA/AU traffic — [EarnifyHub: Blog display ad RPM by niche 2026](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026) (methodology undocumented; treat as indicative)
- Home & Garden/DIY on Mediavine "$22–$35 typical, $28–$42 Q4," "Q4 RPMs spike 30–50%" — [EarnifyHub: Mediavine vs affiliate](https://www.earnifyhub.com/blog/affiliate/mediavine-vs-affiliate-marketing-priority) (labelled "estimates," unverified)
- Mediavine case study: houseplant/gardening blogger Brittany Goldwyn Merth saw +15.5% RPM and +12% revenue from Mediavine's Optimized Ad Experience — [Mediavine case study](https://www.mediavine.com/case-study/optimized-ad-experience-delivers-15-5-rpm-growth/)
- Mediavine homepage cites gardening publisher Old World Garden Farms with a 174% RPM increase in its first year — [Mediavine.com](https://mediavine.com)
- Journey complaints early 2026: RPM near $0.60 after early January 2026; another publisher averaged $0.89 in first week after switching in March 2026 vs ~$10 on AdSense; commenters note January is the seasonal low and Journey RPMs "took roughly a year to reach the 30s" — [r/Blogging snapshot Jan 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1q90ubk/snapshots/2026-01-15T04%3A30%3A10.495303Z); [r/Blogging snapshot Mar 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1rw2u5j/snapshots/2026-03-17T20%3A20%3A18.932354Z) (anecdotal)
- Sample 8-month-old home-decor blog on Mediavine: $49 ad revenue in Aug → $178 in Sept, 170 articles, 743K monthly Pinterest impressions, 16K outbound Pinterest clicks — [Flippa listing](https://flippa.com/14412392-mediavine-approved-home-decor-blog-with-170-articles-178-september-revenue-743k-monthly-pinterest-impressions-and-16k-outbound-clicks-with-strong-growth)

**Seasonality**
- Indexed quarterly RPM (annual avg = 100): Lifestyle Q1 70 / Q2 85 / Q3 80 / Q4 165; Food Q4 130; no separate index for DIY/home/garden — [EarnifyHub RPM by niche](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026)
- Mediavine help: RPM drops during traffic spikes are often because "an increase in a particular source of traffic might yield lower impressions or CPMs" — [Mediavine Help: Why did my RPM drop during a traffic spike](https://help.mediavine.com/why-did-my-rpm-drop-during-a-traffic-spike)
- Q1 is the recognized industry trough for display revenue — [MonetizeMore](https://www.monetizemore.com/blog/how-publishers-combat-q1-ad-revenue-drop/)

**Pinterest vs Google**
- Raptive (qualitative only): search traffic "tends to perform well because readers are actively looking for solutions"; Pinterest's ranking "has improved" — older viral pins brought high pageviews/low RPM, today's users arrive with specific goals and generate higher RPMs; Facebook ranks lower; email ranks highly over time; Pinterest Android-app visits are reported as Direct in GA — [Raptive: Traffic sources and ad revenue](https://raptive.com/resources/traffic-sources)
- Pinterest-majority recipe site: RPM "sitting around $8–10 for months" → "consistently hitting $20" after adding Search Console/sitemap (Google <10% of traffic) and interactive recipe cards that trigger ad refresh — [Reddit repost: How I got my recipe blog to $20 RPM with Pinterest traffic](https://verge.yeeshu.workers.dev/post/how-i-got-my-recipe-blog-to-20-rpm-with-pinterest-traffic-two-things-that-made-t-jYqnG1) (anecdotal, undated year)
- Jon Dykstra (Fat Stacks, Nov 2020): Pinterest 17–20% of his traffic on ~10% of budget; Google revenue "a tad higher but not much"; time-on-site "slightly better" for Google; ranks Google first on consistency, volume, quality — [Fat Stacks: Is Pinterest better than Google traffic?](https://fatstacksblog.com/pinterest-vs-google-traffic/) (2020 — old)
- Travel blogger: "RPM on Google traffic is way higher than on Pinterest" — [Mike and Laura Travel](https://mikeandlauratravel.com/best-platform-rpm-mediavine-blogging/); contradicted by another blogger: "Pinterest and Facebook have had MUCH higher RPMs for me than organic search" — [Cotton Cashmere Cat Hair: Mediavine review](https://cottoncashmerecathair.squarespace.com/blog/mediavine-review-and-income-report)
- Aggregated claim: Pinterest has the lowest bounce rate (48%) and highest pages/session (2.4) among social platforms, "200+ blogs" — [EarnifyHub: Social media traffic 2026](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/social-media-traffic-blogs-2026) (methodology undocumented)
- Single-site example: Google organic ~25% bounce and ~4 pages/session — [Social Media Examiner](https://www.socialmediaexaminer.com/how-to-analyze-site-visitor-engagement-with-google-analytics-reports/) (one site)
- Jenna Campbell (Lantern Lane Designs, DIY/home, 100K+ sessions/mo): ~70% social / ~30% organic, Pinterest main source, on Mediavine, "close to six figures" annually — [Niche Pursuits](https://nichepursuits.com/jenna-campbell-lantern-lane-designs)
- Non-US/UK/CA/AU traffic monetizes 50–80% lower; US traffic earns 3–5× India/SE Asia/E. Europe — [EarnifyHub RPM by niche](https://earnifyhub.com/blog/blogging/blog-display-ad-rpm-by-niche-2026)

### Inferences
- Planning RPMs for this niche: Journey year-1 ~$8–12 (lower in Jan–Feb, higher Oct–Dec); Raptive/Mediavine Official $20–35 blended for a US-majority home/outdoor audience; Q4 roughly 1.5–2× Q1. Outdoor-living content has a spring/summer demand peak (patio, planters, heaters in fall) that partially offsets the Q1–Q3 display trough, but no source quantifies this — see Gaps.
- Pinterest-sourced sessions are not inherently worth less than Google in 2026 per Raptive, but they are single-page, short sessions unless the page has interactive elements (galleries, shop-the-look, calculators) that extend dwell time and trigger ad refresh. The Flippa and recipe-blog data points suggest a Pinterest-only new site should expect ~$8–12 RPM on Journey.
- Ad networks weight the *site-wide* traffic profile; a small Google share (even <10%) appears to lift RPM on Pinterest sessions too (recipe-blog anecdote), consistent with Raptive's "diversified traffic" stance.

### Gaps
- No official Mediavine or Raptive RPM figure for home/garden/outdoor by month or by traffic source; the "Traffic by Source" report exists only inside the publisher dashboard — [Mediavine Help: Traffic by Source report](https://help.mediavine.com/traffic-by-source-report).
- No quantified seasonal index for outdoor-living content specifically (spring/summer patio demand vs Q4 display peak).
- No reliable pages-per-session benchmark comparing Pinterest and Google for home-decor blogs; the EarnifyHub 2.4 figure is undocumented.
- Mediavine Q4 2025 vs Q4 2024 RPM delta was not found; industry data shows display CPMs fell in 2025 ([Pulse/industry summary](https://www.pulse.bot/advertising/news/why-display-cpms-fell-in-2025-and-how-publishers-can-protect-revenues-in-2026-2b447890-4bea-409d-b893-9368d66c29ac/)) and Google search pageviews fell 34% YoY (above).

## Key Question 3: Affiliate programs for outdoor furniture/decor — rates, cookies, networks, approval; Amazon current rates and the 180-day/3-sales rule

### Takeaway
Amazon pays 3% on Furniture, Home, Home Improvement, Lawn & Garden, Outdoors and Tools (4.5% Kitchen) with a 24-hour cookie (90 days for cart adds) and withdraws applications without 3 qualifying sales in 180 days from application. Wayfair (CJ; 7% vs 10% depending on source, 7-day cookie) and Sunnydaze (up to ~11%, 14-day) are the best-paying mass-market patio programs; Article (Awin, 5%, 30 days), Outer (Impact, 10%), Gardener's Supply (Impact, ~6.4–8%, 30 days), Bloomscape (8%, 10–30 days), Etsy (Awin, ~4% US, 30 days) and IKEA (~5%, 30 days) round out the niche; big-box (Home Depot 1-day cookie, ~1–8%; Lowe's 0.8%, 24h; Target 1–8%, 7 days; Walmart 1–4%, 3 days) are poor fits for considered outdoor purchases. Yardbird has no affiliate program found.

### Cited Findings

**Amazon Associates (US)**

- The 3% home/furniture/garden figure is corroborated by [Flywheel Digital](https://flywheeldigital.com/blog/amazon-associates-program-updates) and [AffiliateX](https://affiliatexblocks.com/?p=24733); Sonary lists 4% for the same group — [Sonary](https://sonary.com/reviews/amazon-associates/) — conflict; 3% is the majority and matches the official schedule as summarized by Lasso

## Key Question 7: Realistic month-6 and month-12 revenue stacks at 25k and 100k sessions, by line

### Inferences (model built only from the cited figures above; ranges, not forecasts)

**Sequence that maximizes early revenue (given the 2026 gates):**
1. Months 0–4: no ads (nothing is available except AdSense at ~$2 RPM); build Pinterest + a printable lead magnet; install Grow at month 3 so the 30-day clock is done by month 4; hold the Amazon application until there is traffic (180-day/3-sale rule).
2. Month 4–5: apply to Journey at 1,000 Tier-1 sessions; first Journey payment arrives ~month 7 (Net 65).
3. Month 6–7: once domain is 6 months old and ≥25K pageviews with ≥50% Tier-1 traffic, apply to Raptive (RPM roughly 2–3× Journey); or stay on Journey until $5K/yr and graduate to Mediavine Official at 75%.
4. Month 6+: Wayfair (CJ), Article (Awin), Sunnydaze, Outer (Impact), Etsy (Awin) applications; Amazon once sessions support 3 sales inside 180 days.
5. Month 9–12: first paid printable bundle to the list; first sponsored post pitch at ~25–50K sessions ($500–1,500).

**Month 6 at ~25K sessions (≈30–35K pageviews, Pinterest-majority):**
- Display (Journey, ~$8–12 RPM, single-page sessions): $200–300/mo earned (cash lands month 8–9). If already on Raptive at $20–30 RPM: $500–750.
- Affiliate (Amazon 3% + Wayfair/Article/Sunnydaze on furniture): $50–300.
- Digital products: $0–100 (list is young).
- Sponsored: $0 (occasionally one $300–500 post).
- Stack: ~$300–900/mo earned. Benchmark check: survey average for 25–50K pageviews across all streams is $1,034, and those are mostly older sites; the Flippa month-8 home-decor site made $178.

**Month 12 at ~25K sessions:**
- Display on Raptive/Mediavine Official at $20–35 RPM (Q4 higher): $500–900.
- Affiliate: $150–500.
- Digital products (list ~2–5K): $100–500.
- Sponsored: 0–1 post at $500–1,000.
- Stack: ~$750–2,900/mo; central case ~$1,200–1,800.

**Month 12 at ~100K sessions (≈130K pageviews):**
- Display at $25–36 RPM (Home Décor survey avg $36.23; Raptive avg $34.86): $2,500–3,600 (Q4 $4,000+; Q1 $1,800–2,500).
- Affiliate at ~10% of total (Jenna Campbell pattern) to 20% (if furniture round-ups rank): $400–1,200.
- Digital products: $300–1,000.
- Sponsored: 1–2 posts at $2,000–3,000.
- Stack: ~$3,200–8,800/mo; central case ~$4,000–6,000, ads 60–75%. Benchmark check: survey average for 100–500K pageviews is $9,395 (older, diversified sites); Jenna Campbell at 100K+ sessions is "close to six figures" ≈ $8K/mo after years.

**Month 6 at 100K sessions** is unrealistic for a new site under 2026 conditions (survey: <1-year blogs average 220 pageviews) unless Pinterest goes viral; if it happens, the site would still be gated to Journey (~$11 RPM → ~$1,100/mo) until the domain hits 6 months for Raptive.
