Ideas / roundup post. This is the Pinterest workhorse: a numbered list of concrete, distinct ideas for one specific kind of space or situation.

- 12–20 sections, one idea each. Every idea must be *doable* by the reader described in the keyword and must differ from the others in kind, not degree (not "add plants" and "add more plants").
- Headings are the idea stated specifically ("A fold-down table that bolts to the railing", not "Smart Seating").
- Each section's must_include carries at least one dimension and one price signal (a range, or "under $X").
- Order: cheapest and most universal ideas first, bigger or conditional ideas later.
- One skip_section with 2–4 things not worth doing in this kind of space.
- 2–4 FAQs that match real follow-up searches for the keyword.
- Every section has image: true.
